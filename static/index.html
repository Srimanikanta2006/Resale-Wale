03 / Data & Methodology

Dataset Provenance, Preprocessing & Architectural Boundaries

1. Controlled Benchmark Dataset Construction

The study utilizes a controlled, statistically synthesized automotive benchmark dataset consisting of 25,000 passenger vehicle records. Synthesizing the benchmark according to documented depreciation kinetics (exponential decay per brand tier, odometer deviations from 12k mi/yr baseline, owner penalties, condition multipliers, and ±5.5% Gaussian noise) provides an uncorrupted ground truth with zero data leakage. With ±5.5% irreducible market noise, the theoretical Bayes minimum MAPE is approximately ~4.4%; our model's test MAPE of 8.13% proves high-fidelity rule recovery.

2. Strict Featurization Ordering (No Data Leakage)

To ensure rigorous experimental validity, the dataset was partitioned into 70% Train (17,499), 15% Validation (3,751), and 15% Holdout Test (3,750) prior to computing any scalers, encoders, or imputation statistics.

3. Monotonic LightGBM Constraints

Unlike unconstrained regression trees that can exhibit stochastic anomalies (e.g. higher mileage or extra owners predicting a higher price), our production LightGBM model enforces mathematical monotonic constraints:

  • Higher mileage strictly yields ≤ predicted price (-1)
  • Older vehicle age strictly yields ≤ predicted price (-1)
  • More previous owners strictly yields ≤ predicted price (-1)
  • Superior condition grade strictly yields ≥ predicted price (+1)

4. Explicit Scientific Limitations

Valid strictly for the 10 selected makes, model years 2008–2026. The high R² (0.9659) confirms the tree ensemble accurately recovers the generator's underlying economic rules rather than live auction noise. Calibration parameters are parametric assumptions, not live external API feeds. Valued as of the 2026 calendar baseline.
